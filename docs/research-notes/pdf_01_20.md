Source: "ADHD planner: product design and research" (Sep 28, 2026, 80 pages). This file covers pages 1 to 20 only: section 1 (executive summary), 2 (scope and generalisation), 3 (problem space), 4 (evidence and design principles), 5 (all new-feature tables, 5.1 to 5.7) and 6.1 to 6.2 (day-one medication estimates chart and Australian formulation catalogue). ## 1. Features, screens and flows (with acceptance criteria)

### 1.1 Product core and loop

- Product definition: an Apple-native planner that learns each user's own medication focus window from one-tap check-ins and fills it with work the user chooses; it must survive bad days without shame and let the user flip the whole learned routine to "Lifestyle 2" (for example holidays) and back in one tap. AC: switching Lifestyle is one tap from a surfaced control; switching back restores the previous learned routine unchanged. [p1, p2]
- Core loop: log a dose (Watch, widget, Siri, Action button) -> app shows the usual focus window learned from the user's own check-ins -> solver puts the user's chosen deep work inside the window and body, social and admin tasks outside it -> outcomes feed a behaviour ledger -> nightly on-device learning refines durations, timing and the window. AC: after a dose log the plan shows deep-work blocks inside the window and body, social and admin blocks outside it; every block outcome is written to the ledger; learned values change at most once per night. [p1]
- The app does the planning for the user (planning is the largest measured deficit; recall of a plan once made is normal) and makes time visible at the point of performance (Watch, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island). AC: a full day plan is produced without the user placing tasks by hand (manual edits still allowed); elapsed and remaining time for the current block is visible without opening the app. [p1, p4, p10]
- The product spine copies the active parts of adult ADHD CBT: calendar plus task list, task breakdown, distraction capture, reframing, self-reward and weekly review, in the order capture -> break down -> schedule -> park distractions -> review and reframe. AC: every spine step exists; streaks, points, body doubling, Pomodoro and dopamine menus exist only as optional layers, never required. [p8]
- Every personal rule in the founder's original inventory becomes a configurable policy object with sensible defaults, and the original regime ships as the first profile template. AC: no rule from the original inventory is hard-coded; each maps to a policy object in section 2 of this file. [p2, p3]
- Audience: adults and older teens with diagnosed or suspected ADHD, on or off medication; carers of younger children are out of scope for v1. AC (inferred): planning, check-ins, amnesty and re-entry all work with no medication configured. [p2]

### 1.2 Surfaces and screens

- Glanceable-first surfaces (MVP): widgets, Watch complications, Control Center Controls and Live Activities carry the day; the app is for planning and opening it is the exception. AC: dose log, Now/Next, parking-lot capture and focus-window end are all reachable without opening the app. [p7, p17]
- Screen inventory implied by pages 1 to 20 (derived): Today plan with ideal and baseline views and a focus-window band; Now/Next; dose log with double-dose confirm and cut-off note; medication setup (formulation picker, cut-off, planned breaks, booster, supply, repeats, script expiry, eScript tokens); focus-window detail with confidence, evidence and approve/decline of learned changes; check-ins (focus, brain weather, "ate something?"); welcome back; overdue amnesty; re-plan after a miss; task detail (steps, first step, start-tiny timer, if-then plan, reward pairing); parking lot; waiting-mode card; leave-by; hyperfocus escalation; Lifestyles and Modes; policy editors (PriorityLadder, MovementPolicy, MealPolicy, Routine anchors, SyncPolicy, SavedPlaces); notification budget; Calm and Spark; discreet mode; setup presets; progress; safety signposting; PDF export; shortage mode; Australian admin pack; health admin recall; psychoeducation cards; Simple mode; focus-shield pause screen; Watch app; share extension. [p3, p11 to p18]

### 1.3 Medication and body (PDF section 5.1)

- One-tap dose log (MVP): log from Watch, Control Center, Action button, widget, Siri or an NFC sticker on the bottle. AC: each of the six entry points records a timestamped dose in a single action without opening the app. [p11]
- Double-dose guard (MVP): a second log within the same day shows "Already logged 08:02, 3 h ago" and asks for a deliberate confirm. AC: the second log is not saved until explicitly confirmed; the message shows the first log's clock time and hours elapsed; cancel saves nothing. [p11]
- Dose glance (MVP): the Lock Screen Live Activity and the Watch complication show "Logged 08:02 · 3 h ago · usual focus to ~5:40 pm". AC: shows last log time, time since log and approximate end of the usual focus window, updating without an app launch; shows no drug name when discreet mode is on (inferred from discreet mode). [p12]
- Wake-anchored dose prompt (MVP): the prompt follows actual wake, not a fixed 8:00. Wake signals: (a) the app's own AlarmKit wake alarm being dismissed, (b) a Shortcuts "Wake Up" automation, (c) the first Watch interaction. Apple exposes no API to read the Sleep Schedule. AC: a user who wakes at 10:15 gets the prompt after the 10:15 wake signal, not at 8:00; no Sleep Schedule read is attempted. [p12]
- Personal cut-off display (MVP): the user or prescriber enters a latest-dose time; logging after it shows the neutral note "Later than the cut-off you set. Your prescriber's instructions apply." AC: the log still saves; no advice, alternative time or dose suggestion is shown. [p12]
- Planned breaks (MVP): pre-planned medication-free days are logged as planned, never shown as "missed", and do not pollute learning. AC: a planned-break day produces no missed state, no dose nag and no change to learned window values. [p12]
- Meals and water on the medication timeline (MVP): breakfast prompt in the morning routine, a scheduled lunch "even if not hungry", a snack before the usual end of the focus window, dinner prep, and a one-tap "ate something?". AC: snack lands before the learned window end; lunch is scheduled regardless of appetite; an eat-something prompt fires if nothing is logged (MealPolicy default). [p2, p12]
- Discreet mode (MVP): neutral notification text (example "Morning ✓"), no drug names on the Lock Screen or in calendar titles, alternate app icon, Face ID lock. AC: with discreet mode on, no medication name appears in any notification, Live Activity, widget, complication or mirrored Calendar or Reminders item; opening the app requires Face ID. [p12]
- End-of-focus "landing" alert (v1): 30 min before the end of this user's usual focus window (learned from their own check-ins): wrap up, snack, move hard conversations. Never mentions the medication or suggests a dose. AC: fires at learned window end minus 30 min; copy contains no medication name, dose or timing advice. [p13]
- Supply, repeats and script tracker (v1): counts down stock on each log, forecasts run-out, tracks repeats and script expiry, stores eScript tokens, and books the prescriber visit before the last repeat. AC: stock decrements per logged dose; a run-out date is shown; a prescriber-visit item exists before the final repeat is used. [p13]
- Shortage mode (v1): pharmacy call list with stock notes and call-back reminders, a link to the TGA shortage database, and a prompt to contact the prescriber. [p13]
- Shareable log export (v1): a one-page PDF the user can choose to show their doctor, containing their own logged times, check-ins by hour, appetite, sleep and notes; no interpretation, no effectiveness claims. AC: output is exactly one page, generated only on explicit user action, with no derived judgement text. [p13]
- Cycle overlay (v1.x): optional HealthKit cycle import; focus and mood by cycle phase, framed as "worth raising with your prescriber". AC: off by default; needs HealthKit permission; no dose or timing suggestion. [p13]
- Caffeine log (Later): optional, with a personal cut-off and a sleep comparison. [p13]
- Booster handling: a booster is a second, usually short-acting dose the prescriber has already authorised; the app schedules around it and never suggests one. AC: a booster can be logged and recorded as prescriber-authorised; no UI proposes taking one. [p3]
- Formulation picker from the 2026 Australian catalogue: 5 window formulations, 2 non-window groups (atomoxetine; guanfacine XR and clonidine) and a slot for medicines not available in Australia. AC: choosing a non-window medicine creates no daily focus window; choosing an overseas medicine uses the "catalogue slot for overseas users". [p19, p20]

### 1.4 Time and transitions (PDF section 5.2)

- Now / Next with a shrinking ring (MVP): one current block, one next block, time left as a depleting ring in the Dynamic Island and on the Watch. AC: exactly two items shown; ring fraction equals time remaining over block length; at block end Next becomes Now. [p14]
- Transition haptics (MVP): taps 10, 5 and 1 minutes before a switch, plus a one-tap "park this thought" capture. AC: three haptics fire before every block switch; capture needs one tap. [p8, p14]
- Waiting-mode protocol (MVP): before any appointment, show "You're free until 1:40. Leave-by alarm is set." and offer 2 to 3 side quests sized to the gap. AC: the leave-by alarm is actually scheduled; every offered side quest fits inside the free gap. [p14]
- Leave-by with learned lateness (v1): works back from arrival time using travel ETA plus this user's own measured lateness buffer; leave-by countdowns appear on glance surfaces. [p8, p14]
- Hyperfocus guardrail (v1): during focus blocks, taps with elapsed time; after a set limit escalates to "stand, drink, eat"; warns when the next fixed item is at risk. AC: escalation appears only after the configured limit; the at-risk warning fires when the current overrun would make the next fixed item late. [p14]

### 1.5 Starting and finishing (PDF section 5.3)

- First-step breakdown (MVP): an on-device model splits a task into steps and forces a first physical action under 10 minutes. AC: every breakdown has a first step that is a physical action estimated under 10 minutes; works with no network. [p14]
- Start-tiny timer (MVP): a 2 to 5-minute timer on the first step only. [p14]
- Distraction parking lot (MVP): one tap on the Watch or Lock Screen captures the stray thought and returns to the task. AC: one tap captures and the user lands back on the current task with no navigation. [p14]
- If-then plans (v1): "When I [cue], I will [first action]", with the cue wired to a real trigger (arrive home, lunch logged, Focus on); the technique table says every scheduled task gets one; guided creation outperforms written plans (inferred UI: guided builder, not free text). [p8, p15]
- Movement primer (v1): suggests 10 to 20 minutes of movement right before deep work; a Watch workout confirms it; the solver places the gym before study where possible. [p9, p15]
- Reward pairing (Later): user-built reward list; pair a dull task with a treat that unlocks only during or after it. [p9, p15]
- Working alongside (Later): opt-in, low-pressure presence ("4 people are starting a study block now") or a hand-off to an external body-doubling service (the PDF names Focusmate); no efficacy claims. [p10, p15]
- Timer presets: Pomodoro, "just 5 minutes" and similar are configurable presets, never mandated; self-set breaks are supported. [p10]

### 1.6 Emotion, lapses and coming back (PDF section 5.4)

- Brain-weather check-in (MVP): one tap: low, okay, good. Low collapses the day to its baseline version and halves notifications. AC: tapping Low switches the plan to the baseline set and halves the remaining notification budget for that day. [p15]
- Ideal vs baseline day (MVP): every day is planned twice: the full plan and a minimum viable plan of the 1 to 3 things that keep the week on track; hitting baseline counts as a win. AC: baseline always holds 1 to 3 items; finishing all baseline items is recorded and shown as a win. [p15]
- Overdue amnesty (MVP): one tap reschedules or archives everything overdue; no red counts anywhere. [p11, p15]
- Welcome-back flow (MVP): after 3+ days away: archive stale items, pick 3 things, back in under 30 seconds, no mention of missed days. AC: shown on first open after 3 or more days away; completes in under 30 seconds; no copy mentions missed days or backlog. [p1, p7, p15]
- Forgiving progress (MVP): "5 of the last 7 days", automatic grace days, one-tap fresh start; no daily streaks. [p9, p16]
- Neutral re-plan after a miss (MVP): after every missed block a new slot is offered straight away, with an optional 20-second reframe and a short "what got in the way?" prompt. [p10, p16]
- Safety signposting (MVP): persistently low check-ins surface Lifeline (13 11 14) and a "talk to your GP" prompt; signposting only, never therapy. [p16]

### 1.7 Sleep and routines (PDF section 5.5)

- Wind-down worked back from wake (v1): wind-down time derived from tomorrow's required wake; a protected guilt-free leisure block earlier in the evening. [p16]
- Morning launch on Watch (v1): timed sub-steps (dose, water, breakfast, hygiene, leave-by) with haptic progression. [p16]
- Routine graduation (v1.x): when a routine has run unprompted for weeks, offer to drop its reminders. [p11, p16]
- Routine anchors are tagged calendar-worthy or checklist-only; default is morning and evening checklists only, with nothing trivial on the calendar. AC: checklist-only anchors never create calendar events. [p3]

### 1.8 Life admin (PDF section 5.6)

- Leaving-home check (v1): geofence exit from home prompts "Keys, wallet, meds?". [p16]
- Admin sprints (v1): 10 to 15-minute timed sprints with a templated breakdown for paperwork. [p16]
- Health admin recall (v1): GP script visit before the last repeat; dentist and GP recall intervals. [p17]
- Australian admin pack (v1): PBS Safety Net tracking (resets 1 January), $25 general / $7.70 concession co-payment, and state prescribing notes (for example NSW trained GPs could continue stimulant prescriptions from 1 September 2025; state rules still differ at borders). [p7, p17]
- Bills and trials on the timeline (Later): due dates and free-trial expiry alerts before the charge. [p6, p17]
- Impulse cool-off (Later): share a product into the app; it asks again in 48 to 72 h. [p17]
- Reply-later queue and birthdays (Later): messages saved from any app via the share sheet; birthdays from Contacts with a 7-day gift lead time. [p17]

### 1.9 Attention protection, presentation and setup (PDF section 5.7)

- Notification budget (MVP): default 5 interruptions a day; everything else in 2 to 3 digests; only leave-now, transition and medication alerts break through. [p11, p17]
- Calm and Spark modes: Calm (default, MVP) has a predictable layout, muted colour and no confetti; Spark (v1) has rotating themes and celebrations. [p17]
- Setup presets (MVP): Minimal, Gentle nudges, High structure; working defaults with zero configuration. AC: a user can finish onboarding by choosing one preset and get a working plan with no other settings. [p18]
- Focus filter link (v1): entering a Study Focus starts the planned block and quiets nudges. [p18]
- Micro-psychoeducation (v1): 60-second clinician-reviewed cards in context (sleep, appetite, routines, preparing for appointments). [p18]
- Simple mode (v1.x): an Assistive Access scene with three buttons: Now, Next, Log. [p18]
- Focus shields (v1.x, needs Apple entitlement): Screen Time shields on chosen apps during planned focus blocks, with a 5 to 10-second pause screen showing the current task. [p9, p18]
- Gamification off by default; only light, rotating celebrations; small concrete feedback at completion rather than distant badges. [p10, p11]
- Optional share with a buddy, coach or clinician (phase not stated). [p10]
- Weekly review: the "review and reframe" spine step plus a weekly self-rated functioning check (phase not stated). [p8, p11]
- Semester-shaped onboarding and a weekly check-in for university users (phase not stated). [p8]

### 1.10 Lifestyles, Modes and policy objects

- Lifestyle: a learned behavioural baseline (term time, holidays, shift work); each Lifestyle keeps its own learned routine; the dormant one is frozen, so switching back is instant. AC: nightly learning writes only to the active Lifestyle; switching back restores identical parameters. [p2, p3]
- New Lifestyle 2: starts from the user's own weekend pattern plus three quick answers and is about 70% personalised after a week (detail in PDF section 10). [p2]
- Mode: a temporary dated rule overlay (Exam, Holiday, Sick / Sick Day, Travel) on top of whichever Lifestyle is active; offered when assessments are imported. AC: a Mode applies only between its dates and never edits the underlying Lifestyle. [p3]
- PriorityLadder: reorderable per Lifestyle and per Mode; default Commitments > deadlines > health basics > chosen goals > errands. [p3]
- MovementPolicy: up to three candidate slots; skip conditions offered as toggles (the founder regime skips gym on work, social and exam days). [p3]
- MealPolicy: anchored to a medication event, a clock time or appetite check-ins; composite anchors resolve to the latest of several constraints, which the user picks. [p2, p3]
- SavedPlace: defaults are home, work or school, and one gym or shop; each has an opening-hours source and a learned travel overhead. [p3]
- SyncPolicy per Reminders list and per Calendar; items paired by internal ID, never by title. [p3]
- Hard rules (only the user can change) vs learned preferences (found by the app and approved by the user); every learned number shows its evidence; standing changes need the user's approval. AC: no learned change affects future plans until the user approves it. [p1, p3]
- Day types visible in these pages (for the Medication page "day types"): work day, social day and exam day (MovementPolicy skip conditions), planned medication-free day (planned break), plus Modes and Lifestyles. [p3, p12]

### 1.11 Key flows (derived from the feature tables)

- Dose log flow: any surface -> if already logged today, guard sheet "Already logged HH:MM, N h ago" -> deliberate confirm or cancel -> if after cut-off, neutral note -> save DoseLog -> start or update the dose-glance Live Activity -> decrement supply (v1) -> re-solve the plan around the focus window. [p11, p12, p13]
- Morning flow: wake signal (AlarmKit dismissal, Shortcuts Wake Up, first Watch interaction) -> dose prompt -> morning checklist with breakfast and water -> in v1 the Watch morning launch runs dose, water, breakfast, hygiene, leave-by with haptic progression. [p12, p16]
- Focus block flow: Now/Next ring -> 10/5/1-minute transition haptics with "park this thought" -> hyperfocus taps and escalation (v1) -> outcome to the ledger -> if missed, neutral re-plan with a new slot. [p14, p16]
- Appointment flow: waiting-mode card with free-until time and leave-by alarm -> 2 to 3 side quests -> leave-by alert from ETA plus learned lateness (v1) -> leaving-home "Keys, wallet, meds?" on geofence exit (v1). [p14, p16]
- Bad-day flow: brain weather Low -> baseline plan of 1 to 3 items and halved notifications -> baseline completion counts as a win; persistently low check-ins -> safety signposting. [p15, p16]
- Lapse flow: 3+ days away -> welcome back (archive stale items, pick 3 things, under 30 seconds); overdue amnesty is available at any time with one tap. [p15]
- Learning flow: nightly on-device update -> proposed standing change shown with its evidence and confidence -> user approves or declines; hard rules change only when the user edits them. [p1, p3]
- Lifestyle and Mode flow: one tap switches Lifestyle (dormant one frozen); creating Lifestyle 2 uses the weekend pattern plus three answers; importing assessments offers a dated Exam Mode whose PriorityLadder applies between its dates. [p1, p2, p3]

### 1.12 Global UX acceptance criteria (design principles)

- Point of performance: cues arrive where the task happens (Watch haptic, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island). [p10]
- Micro-sessions: every interaction is designed to take under 3 minutes. [p10]
- Immediate, real rewards: small concrete feedback at completion beats distant badges. [p11]
- Sparse, high-value interruptions: real-time alerts only for leave-now, transition and medication moments; everything else batched. [p11]
- Nothing punishes a miss: no red counts, no broken-streak messages, one-tap amnesty for overdue items. [p11]
- Minimum viable tracking: passive data plus one-tap check-ins by default; tell users when a routine no longer needs reminders. [p11]
- Cognitive accessibility per W3C COGA: plain words, one primary action per screen, nothing that relies on memory, no timeouts, deep personalisation. [p11]
- Visibility beats storage: design for surfaces that stay in view (widgets, Watch face, Live Activities). [p7]
- Calm by default because comorbid anxiety and autism are common and loud, stimulating ADHD apps overwhelm those users. [p7, p17]
- Counter the 2 to 3-week "app feels dead" decay with rotated wording and optional Spark themes, not a static UI with the same prompts forever. [p5, p9, p17]

## 2. Data model (entities, fields, types, retention)

Entity and field names come from the PDF where it gives them; types are proposed (inferred) because the PDF gives none.

- MedicationFormulation (bundled catalogue): id; genericName; auBrands [String]; releaseMechanism (String); dayShape (enum: oneFastHump, twoPeaks, earlyPeakThenLatePeak, oneHump, broadPlateau, noDailyWindow); hasDailyWindow (Bool); typicalDosesPerDay (IR: 2 to 3); strengths (for example dexamfetamine 5 mg tablet, lisdexamfetamine 20 to 70 mg capsules); foodEffect (String); pbsAdultListing (String, as of Feb 2026); availableInAustralia (Bool); dayOneEstimate (four phases, see section 4); sources [URL]. [p19, p20]

- WakeEvent: timestamp; source (alarmKitDismissed | shortcutsWakeUp | firstWatchInteraction). [p12]

- PriorityLadder: scope (lifestyleId, optional modeId); ordered categories, default [commitments, deadlines, healthBasics, chosenGoals, errands]. [p3]
- MovementPolicy: candidateSlots [TimeOfDay] (max 3); skipConditions [toggles, for example workDay, socialDay, examDay]; primerMinutes 10 to 20. [p3, p9]
- Mode: type (exam | holiday | sick | travel); startDate; endDate; ruleOverrides (including its own PriorityLadder); origin (manual | assessmentImport). [p3]

- Routine and RoutineAnchor: name and time (for example dinner 8:30pm, 11pm reflection, watch charging, sleep); tag (calendarWorthy | checklistOnly); checklist (morning | evening); unpromptedRunCount and graduationOffered state. [p3, p16]
- SyncPolicy: targetType (calendar | remindersList); targetId; mapping internal ID to Apple item identifier; never matched by title. [p2, p3]
- SavedPlace: type (home | workOrSchool | gym | shop); location; openingHoursSource; learnedTravelOverhead (minutes); geofence (home, for the leaving-home check). [p3, p16]
- Task: id; title; category (deepWork | body | social | admin | errand); ladder category; deadline; learnedDuration; steps [Step]; firstStep (physical action under 10 min); ifThenPlan (cue trigger, firstAction); rewardPairing (optional); status (planned | done | missed | overdue | archived); inBaseline (Bool). [p1, p8, p14, p15]
- ScheduledBlock: taskId; start; end; isFocusBlock; mirroredItemId; transition haptic times. [p1, p14]
- DayPlan: date; idealBlocks; baselineItems (1 to 3); brainWeather; activeLifestyleId; activeModeIds. [p15]
- BehaviourLedgerEntry: blockId; planned start and end; actual start and end; done (Bool); onTime (Bool); latenessMinutes; whatGotInTheWay (optional). [p1, p10, p11, p14]
- ParkingLotItem: text; capturedAt; source (watch | lockScreen | transitionPrompt); linkedBlockId. [p14]
- ProgressState: rolling 7-day count; graceDays; freshStartAt. [p16]
- NotificationPolicy: dailyCap (default 5); digestsPerDay (2 to 3); breakthroughCategories (leaveNow, transition, medication); learnedSendTimes; sendNothing (Bool); wordingVariants for rotation; halvedToday (Bool, set by brain weather Low). [p9, p15, p17]
- Supply: unitsOnHand; decrement per log; runOutForecastDate; repeatsRemaining; scriptExpiry (date); eScriptTokens (sensitive); prescriberVisitDue (before the last repeat). [p13]
- PharmacyContact (shortage mode): name; phone; stockNotes; callBackReminderAt. [p13]
- PBSSafetyNet: yearToDateTotal; resets 1 January; coPaymentGeneral $25; coPaymentConcession $7.70; concession (Bool); state. [p17]
- HealthAdminRecall: kind (gpScriptVisit | dentist | gpRecall); interval; nextDue. [p17]
- CycleData (v1.x): phase imported from HealthKit, opt-in. [p13]
- CaffeineLog (Later): time; personal cut-off. [p13]
- Later-phase entities: RewardItem; ReplyLaterItem (shared content, source app); Birthday (from Contacts, 7-day lead); BillOrTrial (due date, trial expiry); ImpulseItem (shared product, askAgainAt 48 to 72 h). [p15, p17]
- Settings: preset (minimal | gentleNudges | highStructure); presentation (calm | spark); discreetMode (neutralText, alternateIcon, faceIDLock); simpleMode; focusShields (selected apps, pause 5 to 10 s); focusFilterLink (Study Focus); hyperfocusLimit; timerPresets. [p12, p17, p18]
- PsychoEdCard: 60-second card; clinician-reviewed; context tags (sleep, appetite, routines, preparing for appointments). [p18]
- LearnedParameter: value; evidence; confidence; state (proposed | approved | declined); provenance (hardRule | learnedPreference). [p1, p3]
- ProfileTemplate: the original regime as the first template and the main test fixture (values in section 4). [p2]
- Source of truth: the app owns its own data model; Apple Calendar and Reminders are mirrored outputs and inputs, never the source of truth. [p2]
- Storage constraints: personal health information may not be stored in iCloud (App Review), and apps can read but not write Health doses, so dose logs live in the app's own store (workarounds in PDF section 11). [p1]
- Retention stated in these pages: the dormant Lifestyle is kept frozen (not updated, not deleted); planned-break days are kept but excluded from learning; amnesty and welcome back archive stale items rather than delete them. No retention periods are given in pages 1 to 20. [p2, p12, p15]

## 3. Backend services, APIs, jobs, integrations

- Canonical app-owned store with two-way mirroring to Apple Calendar and Reminders under SyncPolicy, paired by internal ID, never by title. [p2, p3]
- Nightly on-device learning job ("small, local Bayesian arithmetic") that refines durations, timing and the focus window; it must tolerate iOS giving no reliable background time (workaround in PDF section 11). [p1]
- Scheduling solver: inputs are the learned focus window, task categories, PriorityLadder, MealPolicy, MovementPolicy, Routine anchors, SavedPlaces, active Lifestyle and Modes, and fixed commitments; outputs an ideal plan plus a baseline plan and re-plans after misses. [p1, p3, p15, p16]
- On-device model for task breakdown that forces a first physical step under 10 minutes. [p14]
- HealthKit reads: medication doses (read only; writing is impossible), cycle data (v1.x, optional), Watch workouts (movement primer confirmation). [p1, p13, p15]
- Wake detection combining AlarmKit alarm dismissal, a Shortcuts "Wake Up" automation (an App Intent the user adds to that automation, inferred) and the first Watch interaction. [p12]
- NFC sticker on the medication bottle as a dose-log trigger. [p11]
- Travel ETA source for leave-by (MapKit ETA, inferred) plus the learned per-user lateness buffer. [p14]
- Geofencing on the home SavedPlace for the leaving-home check, and arrival triggers for if-then cues. [p15, p16]
- Contacts read for birthdays; share-sheet extension for reply-later messages and impulse cool-off products (both Later). [p17]
- Screen Time shields for focus blocks (v1.x, Apple entitlement required). [p18]
- Focus filter integration so a Study Focus starts the planned block and quiets nudges (v1). [p18]
- TGA medicine shortage database link from shortage mode (v1). [p13]

## 4. Algorithms and parameters (every number)

- Focus (effect) window model: per medication, four points learned per person (onset, peak, plateau end, wear-off); day one uses label-range estimates that are user-editable, refined by check-ins and replaced by the user's own data within a week or two; the window is confidence-labelled. [p1, p2, p18]
- Label vs reality: 57% of 1,327 lisdexamfetamine users report 7 hours or less against a label showing effect to 14 h, so learning must be able to shrink a window far below the label. [p1, p18]
- Day-one estimates, hours after the logged dose (phase edges measured from the chart to about 0.1 h; fully-off ranges are printed on it). Methylphenidate IR: kicking in 0.3 to 1.25, usually working 1.25 to 2.75, fading 2.75 to 3, fully off 3 to 5. [p19]
- Dexamfetamine IR: kicking in 0.3 to 1.25, usually working 1.25 to 3.75, fading 3.75 to 4, fully off 4 to 6. [p19]
- Ritalin LA: kicking in 0.5 to 1.75, usually working 1.75 to 6.5, fading 6.5 to 7, fully off 7 to 9; possible dip at about 4 to 4.5 h between the two bead releases (two peaks about 4 h apart). [p19, p20]
- Concerta (OROS): kicking in 1 to 2, usually working 2 to 10.5, fading 10.5 to 12, fully off 12 to 14; a small early peak at 1 to 2 h, then rising to its maximum at 6 to 10 h. [p19]
- Lisdexamfetamine: kicking in 1 to 3.5, usually working 3.5 to 11, fading 11 to 12, fully off 12 to 15; about 1 h later with food; adult effect significant at every point from 2 to 14 h, but 14 h was the last point tested. [p19, p20]
- Fully-off ranges deliberately run past labelled durations (Ritalin LA label 6 to 8 h, Concerta label 12 h) because lab studies stop measuring at their last time point; chart headline "IR doses last about 3 to 6 hours; Concerta and Vyvanse labels reach 12+". [p19]
- Food and other modifiers: methylphenidate IR high-fat meal raises exposure about 25%; Ritalin LA high-fat meal gives a longer lag and a second peak about 25% lower, and 40% alcohol dumps 98% of the dose in the first hour; Concerta no food effect; dexamfetamine IR levels lowered by vitamin C and fruit juice, raised by antacids; lisdexamfetamine peak about 1 h later with food; atomoxetine peak delayed about 3 h by a high-fat meal; guanfacine avoid high-fat meals. [p19, p20]
- Release mechanics: Ritalin LA 50% instant and 50% delayed beads; Concerta osmotic pump with a 22% instant coat; IR tablets 2 to 3 doses a day; lisdexamfetamine is a prodrug converted in the blood (smooth broad plateau); dexamfetamine blood levels outlast the felt effect; atomoxetine, guanfacine XR and clonidine build over weeks with no daily window (guanfacine: possible drowsiness). [p19, p20]
- PBS adult listing (Feb 2026): methylphenidate IR yes, no age limit; Ritalin LA yes, after IR response shown; Concerta only ages 6 to 18 diagnosed in childhood; dexamfetamine IR yes, no age limit; lisdexamfetamine yes, including adult diagnosis; atomoxetine ages 6 to 18 only; guanfacine and clonidine check current listing; overseas medicines n/a. [p19, p20]
- Learning: local Bayesian updates run nightly; each learned number carries its evidence; standing changes need approval; planned-break days are excluded; the dormant Lifestyle is frozen. [p1, p2, p12]
- Lifestyle 2 cold start: seeded from the user's weekend pattern plus three quick answers; target about 70% personalised after 7 days. [p2]
- Coupled re-planning chain: a late wake shifts the dose, the logged dose shifts the focus window, and the window shifts lunch and bedtime, so an upstream change must re-solve everything downstream. [p7]
- Solver placement rules: deep work inside the focus window; body, social and admin tasks outside it; hard tasks away from wear-off and rebound (the mid-to-late afternoon crash of about 1 hour); movement or gym before study where possible; snack before the usual window end; lunch scheduled even if not hungry; composite meal anchor equals the latest of its constraints; PriorityLadder order resolves conflicts; up to 3 movement slots with skip toggles; checklist-only anchors never go on the calendar. [p1, p3, p6, p9, p12]
- Double-dose guard: triggers on a second log within the same day; message shows the first-log clock time and hours elapsed. [p11]
- Cut-off check: log time later than the latest-dose time shows the neutral note and changes nothing else. [p12]
- Landing alert time: learned focus-window end minus 30 minutes. [p13]
- Transition haptics: 10, 5 and 1 minute before each block switch. [p14]
- Waiting mode: free gap is the time until leave-by; offer 2 to 3 side quests, each sized to fit the gap. [p14]
- Leave-by time: arrival time minus travel ETA minus the user's measured lateness buffer. [p14]
- Hyperfocus guardrail: elapsed-time taps during focus blocks; escalation after a set limit (default not stated); at-risk warning for the next fixed item. [p14]
- Fixed durations: first step under 10 min; start-tiny timer 2 to 5 min; movement primer 10 to 20 min; admin sprints 10 to 15 min; optional reframe 20 s; psychoeducation card 60 s; focus-shield pause 5 to 10 s. [p9, p14, p15, p16, p18]
- Brain weather Low: plan switches to baseline and the notification budget is multiplied by 0.5. [p15]
- Lapse parameters: baseline plan 1 to 3 items; welcome back after 3+ days away, pick 3 things, under 30 seconds; re-entry after two weeks away also under 30 seconds. [p7, p15]
- Forgiving progress: rolling 7-day window ("5 of the last 7 days"); automatic grace days (count not stated); one-tap fresh start. [p16]
- Notifications: default 5 interruptions a day; 2 to 3 digests; breakthrough only for leave-now, transition and medication; learned send times; a "send nothing" option; rotated wording. [p9, p17]
- Supply: stock decremented on each log; run-out forecast; prescriber visit before the last repeat. [p13]
- Life admin: impulse cool-off re-ask after 48 to 72 h; birthday gift lead 7 days; PBS Safety Net resets 1 January; co-payment $25 general, $7.70 concession. [p17]
- Interaction budget: under 3 minutes per interaction (reference: about 3.4 opens a week of about 3.4 minutes each, 52.7% retained at week 7). [p10]
- Original regime (first template and main test fixture): productive window T to T+9h after medication; lunch no earlier than T+5h; work-day lunch = max(T+5h, home time); priority work and exam prep > uni > social > gym > meals > errands; gym at 11am or 3pm, skipped on work, social and exam days; exam period 9 to 27 November; dinner 8:30pm; 11pm reflection; watch charging; sleep; Norwest gym and nearby errands as SavedPlaces. [p2, p3]

[p4, p5]

[p5, p6, p7]

## 12. Edge cases, failure modes, mitigations

- Drift-off after two weeks (apps feel dead after 2 to 3 weeks; day-30 retention 3.3%): welcome back, forgiving progress, rotated wording, Spark and under-30-second re-entry (full mitigations in PDF section 13). [p2, p5, p7]
- The scheduler over-packs the day or makes one bad move: ideal vs baseline day, approval for standing changes, evidence shown, neutral re-plan. [p1, p2, p15]
- Setup burden: presets with zero-configuration defaults and minimum viable tracking. [p2, p11, p18]
- Regulatory or reputational exposure around stimulants: red lines, neutral copy, discreet mode, legal review. [p1, p2, p12]
- "Did I already take it?": the double-dose guard. [p5, p11]
- Legitimate repeat doses (IR taken 2 to 3 times a day, authorised boosters) will trip a same-day guard every time, so guard rules must respect the recorded regimen (inferred). [p3, p11, p19]
- A late dose after the cut-off saves with the neutral note only. [p12]
- Variable or late wake (delayed sleep in about 3 in 4 people): wake-anchored prompt from three signals; no Sleep Schedule API exists. [p4, p12]
- A user with no Watch who does not use the app's alarm or the Shortcut has no wake signal; a fallback is needed (inferred). [p12]
- Drug holidays are planned breaks, never missed and never used for learning. [p12]
- Label vs real duration mismatch (57% report 7 hours or less): learned window replaces labels within 1 to 2 weeks and shows a confidence label. [p1, p18]
- Ritalin LA dip at about 4 to 4.5 h: a four-point window (onset, peak, plateau end, wear-off) cannot express two peaks, so a dip field may be needed (inferred). [p2, p19]
- Food shifts timing (lisdexamfetamine about 1 h later with food; atomoxetine peak about 3 h later after a high-fat meal). [p19, p20]
- Non-window medicines (atomoxetine, guanfacine XR, clonidine) have no daily window; the planner must still produce a plan. [p20]
- Rebound crash mid-to-late afternoon for about 1 hour: landing alert and hard tasks moved away from it. [p6, p13]
- Hyperfocus blows past the next commitment: guardrail with the at-risk warning. [p4, p14]
- Waiting mode loses the whole morning: waiting-mode protocol with side quests. [p4, p14]
- Appetite suppression and dry mouth: lunch even if not hungry, snack before window end, water. [p5, p12]
- Supply shortages: shortage mode and the supply tracker. [p6, p13]
- Calendar exposure (calendars are never end-to-end encrypted): no drug names in calendar titles under discreet mode. [p12]
- Low days: brain weather Low gives the baseline plan and halved notifications; persistent lows trigger signposting. [p15, p16]
- Overdue pile: one-tap amnesty and no red counts. [p15]
- Missed block: a new slot straight away and an optional reframe. [p16]
- Anxious or autistic users overwhelmed by stimulation: Calm is the default. [p7, p17]
