# Build spec: shared context for writers

This folder becomes the Figma "Build spec" page: the single document someone could use to build the finished product without asking questions. Write for an engineer and a designer who have never seen this project.

## The product

- **Focus** (repo folder "ADHD Slayer"): an iPhone day planner for adults (18+) with ADHD. Australia first, English (en-AU).
- It plans each day around the person's own medication rhythm and their own preferences. It never tells anyone what to take, how much or when.
- Everything smart runs on the iPhone with Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models, iOS 26). iPhones that can't run Apple Intelligence can't use the app. There is no App Store device key for this, so the app checks at launch and shows a kind "this iPhone can't run Focus" screen.
- Look: quiet and elegant. Dark first (dark mode is the default, light is a toggle). In dark mode the frosted backdrop is dimmed by 50% by default (Customise, Look, "Darkness", 0 to 80%). Serif headings (New York), small type, lots of space, everything left aligned. Liquid Glass chrome. Frosted photo backdrops (a dusk façade for dark, a baroque interior for light). One idea per page; pages snap like reels with page dots.
- Voice: a warm, slightly funny friend who also has ADHD. Plain words, short sentences, never clinical, never shaming. No dashes as punctuation. Every user-facing sentence has 100 hand-written variants that rotate daily; serious, legal and button text never rotates and never jokes.
- Vibrations everywhere (Core Haptics), with an off switch and a strength slider.

## Decisions already made (build to these)

- **You tab, kept clutter free (owner's instruction, 30 Sep):** page 1 is only your name plus two calm rows, Medication and Personalise. Page 2 is support ("If things feel like too much"). Page 3, the final page two swipes in, is "The fiddly bits": four quiet rows (One-press logging, Customise, Settings, Your own AI) and nothing else. Every complicated control lives one level deeper inside Settings or Customise, never on the tab pages. The final page can't be removed in Customise, so Settings is always reachable.

- **First launch:** device check, then start animation, greeting, "The honest bit" (not a medical device: doesn't diagnose, treat, monitor or prevent; never says what, how much or when; talk to your doctor or pharmacist; crisis 000 and Lifeline 13 11 14), then account (18+ toggle; Sign in with Apple, Google or email magic link), then the Plus trial offer before the free plan, then straight to the home page with skippable coach marks (not a guided setup).
- **Payments:** Apple only (StoreKit 2). Products `au.adhdslayer.focus.plus.monthly` and `au.adhdslayer.focus.plus.yearly`, one subscription group, introductory offer free for 1 month. Apple's trial periods are fixed (1 month, not 31 days), so copy says "a month". Trial message: ADHD takes time to understand and so does knowing whether an app helps; if it doesn't, just cancel. Reminder 2 days before the trial ends.
- **Free vs Plus:** free has 3 kinds of day and 5 activity categories; Plus is unlimited. Everything else is free.
- **Medication page (You, first page):** medications with name, strength, kind (All day, Short-acting, Wind-down, Something else), colour, "working well after", "stops helping after", "you can usually sleep after" sliders, optional learning from check-ins. Kinds of day: named, with doses at times (e.g. 5 mg short-acting, then Vyvanse, then another short-acting), automatic or own productive windows, low-focus gaps between windows, a wind-down medicine (never called "sleep medication") that ends focus earlier with a buffer, and "applies when" (weekdays and/or a date range such as school holidays). Today's kind of day: manual choice, else the shortest matching date range, else weekday match, else the first.
- **Home page:** "Today, in five" (five short priorities written on the phone by Apple Intelligence, with a ranked fallback), then today's medication as one slim row: two medication buttons (tap to log that dose, hold for another time) and a more button that opens every dose for today with "Log all".
- **Logging:** animated celebration and vibration when logged, with an off switch. One-press logging with the Action button (App Intent), suggested right after the first medication is saved and always in Settings. Back Tap and Siri as alternatives. A Control Center control needs a widget extension.
- **Personalise (You, first page):** activity categories (5 free) with preferred windows as hours after the first dose; eating, social and alone windows; on-device detection of plans with other people in the calendar and a suggestion to ask the person to move it into the social window (toggle to switch off; the person sends the message themselves).
- **Customise (You, last page, "The fiddly bits"):** opens to a short list of categories; each opens its own page of controls: pages (add, remove, reorder per tab), tabs (rename, hide Plan or Rhythm, reorder), look (light/dark/match, backdrops, darkness in dark mode (default 50%), blur, mist, grain, accent, heading typeface, text size, roundness, page markers), voice (humour level, daily variety, nickname), feel (vibrations, strength, slider ticks, logging celebration, still motion), home (3 or 5 priorities, meds before "more", show source, bottom bar content), focus mode (timer style, first step). Warning: "Careful, this might become your hyperfixation and eat the next six hours."
- **Settings (You, last page):** opens to a short, calm list: one-press logging near the top, then categories that each open their own page: account (sign out, delete account), Plus (manage, restore, redeem code), look, privacy (discreet mode, what leaves the phone, your own AI), permissions, export and delete data, safety and legal, support.
- **Bring your own AI:** optional, off by default, OpenAI, Anthropic or a custom HTTPS endpoint (Gemini excluded). Key only in the iPhone Keychain; requests go straight from the phone to the provider. Only task names and times, and only if allowed. Never medication, doses, check-ins, health data, name or account. A factual warning before connecting (we're not responsible for its answers; their terms apply; some free plans train on data). Nothing in Focus depends on it.
- **Health data:** stored on the iPhone with complete file protection; never in iCloud (App Store 5.1.3(ii)); moving to a new phone uses our own end-to-end encrypted sync that stores ciphertext only (Australian hosting). Separate explicit consent before storing medication details.
- **Compliance wording:** never suggest, calculate or prompt dose actions. Avoid "due", "onset", "wear off", "booster", "predicts", "optimal". Notifications never name a condition or medicine. Fixed referral line: "That's one for your prescriber or pharmacist." Crisis: 000, Lifeline 13 11 14 (AU), 988 (US).
- **Known constraints to state plainly:** 31 days isn't possible (Apple offers 1 month); requiring an account first carries an App Review risk under 5.1.1(v) (mitigation: it genuinely powers encrypted backup and moving phones; sign-in is fast; deletion in app); Google sign-in needs a client ID; Sign in with Apple needs the capability and signing; StoreKit needs App Store Connect products (a local `Products.storekit` exists for testing); Control Center controls, widgets and Live Activities need extensions.

## How to write

- Work on your own: do not start sub-agents. Read the extraction notes in `docs/research-notes/` first and open the originals in `~/Downloads/` only to fill gaps.

- Output markdown only, into this folder, with exactly the file names you are given. Don't change app code.
- Structure: one `# ` title per file, `## ` sections, `### ` subsections, `- ` bullets (nest at most one level with two spaces). Tables allowed with at most 5 columns. Short code blocks allowed for schemas and endpoints.
- Be specific and buildable: field names and types, numbers, limits, endpoints, states, error cases, acceptance criteria. Prefer many short bullets over paragraphs. One bullet is at most about two lines.
- Plain, humane English. **No em dashes, no en dashes, no spaced hyphens as punctuation** (use full stops, commas and colons). No emoji.
- **Exclude** anything about competitors and any debate about whether the app is a medical device. Only include what's needed to build and run the product (the concrete rules the app follows are fine).
- Match the code where it exists, and mark anything not built yet as "To build".
